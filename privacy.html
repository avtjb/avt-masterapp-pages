AVT Master App - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

AVT Master App ("the App") is an internal operational tool developed by Resolution Midlands Ltd ("AVT", "we", "us") for use by our own employees and engineers. It is not intended for use by, or distribution to, the general public.

Information we collect

  • Microsoft account information. The App requires sign-in with your company Microsoft account (via Microsoft Entra ID). We access your name, email address, and security group membership to authenticate you and determine which parts of the App you have permission to use.
  • Location. With your permission, the App uses your device's location to display local weather on the home screen. Location data is not stored or shared beyond this feature.
  • Camera and photo library. The App uses the camera to scan barcodes and capture photos of equipment and rooms, and can access your photo library to attach existing photos to equipment or room records. Photos you capture or select are uploaded to our company Microsoft SharePoint environment as part of your work records.
  • NFC/RFID. The App uses your device's NFC reader to scan RFID tags on rooms and equipment, used to identify records within the App. No personal data is stored on or read from the tags themselves.
  • Microphone. The App requests microphone permission only because it is required by the underlying camera-scanning library; the App does not record or use audio.

How we use this information

All data collected through the App is used solely to operate AVT's internal room-check, equipment-tracking, and asset-management processes for our clients. Data is stored within our organisation's Microsoft 365 / SharePoint environment and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties outside of the Microsoft services we use to run the App.

Data retention

Data collected through the App is retained in accordance with AVT's internal data retention policies and our contractual obligations to our clients.

Your rights

As the App is used by our own employees in the course of their employment, any requests relating to personal data collected through the App should be directed to your line manager or the contact below.
