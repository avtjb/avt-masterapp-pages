Privacy Policy — AVT Masterapp

Effective from 3 September 2026 · for TestFlight beta testers of the AVT Masterapp iOS app
You're testing AVT Masterapp through TestFlight — an internal tool built for AV-Team engineers and staff, not available to the general public. This policy explains what the app does with your information while you're beta testing it, and what Apple's TestFlight platform itself collects on top of that.

1Who this policy covers

This policy is issued by Resolution Midlands Ltd ("the Company", "we", "us") and covers anyone using AVT Masterapp as a TestFlight beta tester — an AV-Team employee, an approved contractor, or (for App Store review purposes) an Apple reviewer using a demo account.

2What the app does

AVT Masterapp is a launcher for a small number of internal work modules. What you see depends on which security groups your Microsoft 365 account belongs to:

  • LBG Room Checker — meeting room AV equipment inspections, fault logging with photos, and RFID/NFC tag assignment for room identification.
  • Warehouse Asset — scanning and tracking warehouse assets and equipment.
  • Holiday Request — submitting annual, sick, or unpaid leave requests and viewing manager approval status.

The Launcher's Apps screen also shows local weather, using your device's approximate location.

3Information we collect

Your account identity

The app signs in using your AV-Team Microsoft 365 account. We receive your name, email address, and which internal security groups you belong to — this determines what the app shows you. We never see or store your password; sign-in is handled directly by Microsoft.

What you enter or capture in the app

  • Room inspection results, fault descriptions, and photos you take or attach of equipment (LBG Room Checker)
  • RFID/NFC tag identifiers read when assigning a tag to a room
  • Asset scan records (Warehouse Asset)
  • Leave dates, leave type, and any notes you enter (Holiday Request)

Device permissions

  • Camera — to scan barcodes and capture photos of rooms and equipment.
  • Photo library — to attach existing photos to a record, and to save photos you've captured back to your library.
  • NFC — to read RFID tags on rooms and equipment.
  • Location (when in use) — to show local weather on the Apps screen. Not used for any other purpose, and not attached to anything you submit.
  • Microphone — requested only because it's required by the underlying camera-scanning library; the app does not record or use audio.

Each permission is used only when you actively use the relevant feature — nothing is captured in the background.

4How we use it

Information is used only to operate the features described above — for example, so a fault you log is visible to whoever schedules the repair, or so your manager can approve your leave request. We do not use your information for advertising, and the app does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5Where your data is stored

App data is stored in the Company's own Microsoft 365 / SharePoint Online environment — it does not pass through or get stored on any third-party server we operate. The app itself is distributed via TestFlight, which is subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

6What TestFlight itself collects

Separately from anything AVT Masterapp does, Apple's TestFlight platform collects its own usage and diagnostic data from beta testers — for example crash logs and, if you choose to send one, your written feedback on a build. This is collected and controlled by Apple, not by us; see Apple's privacy policy for details on how they handle it.

7Who can see it

Only AV-Team staff with appropriate access to the relevant SharePoint site can see the data you enter — access follows the same internal security groups that control what you see in the app. For App Store review purposes, a dedicated demo account with sample data may be provided to Apple's review team; this account is separate from real staff data.

8Your rights

As a UK-based Company, we handle personal data in line with UK GDPR. You can ask to see what's held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask questions about how long it's kept — contact IT using the details below. Leave and sickness records are also subject to the Company's normal HR data retention policies.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or the beta program changes. The effective date at the top of the page will always reflect the most recent version.
