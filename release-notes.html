Release Notes

What's New

A quick, plain-English log of what's changed in the app over time — newest first. For step-by-step help, see the Full App Guide.

12 Sep 2026
  • LBG - Site ManagementNew browser-based dashboard for site managers — no app install needed. See room status and open issues across the whole estate, manage site coverage, and report faults, all in one place.
  • LBG - Site ManagementThe Overview tab groups rooms by building — tap a building to expand it, use the search box to jump straight to a room, and each building's rooms are sorted alphabetically and split into Hubs, MTRs, Event Spaces, and Other.
  • LBG - Site ManagementOpen Issues are grouped by building too — tap any issue to open that room's full details and clear the fault from there.
  • LBG - Site ManagementCoverage and Report Fault now only appear for managers, rather than showing to everyone.
  • LBG - Site ManagementAdded a note on Open Issues that newly granted manager access can take a few minutes to show up.
10 Sep 2026
  • LBG - Room CheckerAdded a "Swift Pass" button to the Inspection Checklist — marks everything as Pass in one tap, with a confirm step first.
  • LBG - Room CheckerA fault found during a room check now clears itself automatically once that item passes on a later check — no need to use Clear Fault for those any more. Faults reported via Shoulder Tap or a Computacenter email still need Clear Fault as before.
  • LBG - Room CheckerRoom Photos now has a slot for photographing the RFID tag's mounting location.
  • LBG - Room Checker"Faults on your site(s)" now refreshes automatically and has a manual Refresh button — fixes a site-coverage change not showing up until you signed out and back in.
  • AVT - Holiday RequestYour balance now also shows Sick and Unpaid days used/pending, not just Annual Leave remaining.
  • LBG - Room CheckerFaults originally reported via the Inspection Checklist no longer show a Clear Fault button — labelled "Room Check" instead, since they clear automatically once that item passes on a recheck. Shoulder Tap and Computacenter-email faults still use Clear Fault as before.
  • LBG - Room Checker"Faults on your site(s)" dropped its explanatory text and now always shows, even with no sites currently allocated to you.
  • LBG - Room CheckerRoom checks now record whether the room was opened via an RFID/NFC tap or a manual search.
  • AVT - App-wideFixed a bug where sign-in could hang indefinitely on a slow or unreliable network — it now fails gracefully after a short wait so you can just try again.
  • AVT - Holiday RequestStrengthened how your balance check verifies who's asking — now checked directly against your Microsoft sign-in rather than a submitted address.
  • AVT - Holiday RequestFixed the "Couldn't check your entitlement" error some of you saw after the security update above — the fix itself was sound, but it was sending its proof-of-sign-in in a way our platform's own gateway quietly intercepted before it ever reached our system. It now goes through correctly. Sorry for the trouble — please let us know if you still see it.
9 Sep 2026
  • AVT - Holiday RequestApproved leave no longer shows your reason on the shared team calendar — just the dates and leave type. Your own personal Outlook invite already worked this way.
8 Sep 2026
  • LBG - Room CheckerFault notes now save your edits properly and get a date/time stamp when updated.
  • LBG - Room CheckerChecklist items renamed for consistency (e.g. "Camera operational").
  • LBG - Room CheckerClear Fault screen relabelled and given a bigger notes box.
  • AVT - Holiday RequestRequest cards are now collapsible — tap to see approval details.
  • AVT - Holiday RequestNotification emails now come from a dedicated Holiday Request mailbox.
  • LBG - Room CheckerFault records now show the plain device name (e.g. "Camera") instead of the checklist wording, so it's clearer at a glance.
  • AVT - App-wideAdded this page — a "See What's New" link now sits in Help alongside the Full App Guide.
  • AVT - App-wideStarted laying the groundwork for an Android release (no build available yet).
  • AVT - App-wideThe support email address is now tappable everywhere it appears — opens your mail app directly.
  • AVT - App-wide"Open Full App Guide" and "See What's New" are now proper buttons, moved to the top of Help for easier access.
  • AVT - App-wideThe app is now deployed company-wide via TestFlight — email tech@av-team.co.uk to request an invite. This is the only way to get the app for now, while the App Store listing itself is still waiting on Apple's review.
7–8 Sep 2026
  • LBG - Room CheckerOpen faults are now automatically detected and pre-filled when you re-check a room, and each fault gets its own tracking number.
  • LBG - Room CheckerFixed a fault's "reported" date resetting every time the room was re-checked.
  • LBG - Room CheckerFixed Report Fault sometimes creating duplicate entries for the same ongoing issue.
  • LBG - Room CheckerA passing check no longer auto-clears an unrelated open fault — only Clear Fault does. Added "Replacement" as a resolution type.
  • LBG - Room CheckerFault-resolved emails now show who resolved it and how.

  • LBG - Room CheckerNew Coverage tab for managers to reassign site cover (e.g. for sickness).
  • LBG - Room CheckerRFID tag placement guidance added to the Help Guide, with a reminder before scanning a room with no tag yet.
  • LBG - Room CheckerVarious fixes to room matching and site-coverage lookups.
