Adding your team calendar

Your team's calendar is shared, not automatic — add it once in Outlook and it stays there. Steps below for desktop, web, and mobile.

Outlook desktop (Windows & Mac)

New Outlook
  1. Switch to the Calendar view (icon in the left rail).
  2. Click Add calendar at the top of the calendar list.
  3. Choose Add from directory.
  4. Type your team's calendar name from the table above (e.g. AVT-CALENDAR-LBG-Support) and select it.
  5. It appears in your calendar list with a coloured checkbox — untick it any time to hide it, it stays added.
Still on classic Outlook?Home tab → Open Calendar → From Address Book → search the same name → OK. It lands under "Shared Calendars" in the left pane.
🌐

Outlook on the web

outlook.office.com
  1. Sign in at outlook.office.com and open the Calendar icon in the left rail.
  2. Click Add calendar above your calendar list.
  3. Choose Add from directory, search your team's calendar name, and select it.
📱

Outlook Mobile

iOS & Android
  1. Open the Outlook app and tap the Calendar icon at the bottom.
  2. Tap the + in the top corner (or tap your calendar list at the top-left) to Add calendar.
  3. Choose Add from directory, search your team's calendar name, and tap it.
  4. It joins your calendar list with its own colour — tap it any time to toggle it on or off.
Don't see "Add from directory"?Update the Outlook app to the latest version from the App Store / Play Store — older versions place it under a slightly different menu.
Common questions
I added it but can't see any events

Give it a minute to sync, then pull to refresh (mobile) or restart Outlook. If it's still empty, you may not have permission yet — check with IT.

Can I add both calendars?

Yes — if you're in a group for both teams, add both the same way. Nothing stops you seeing more than one.

Do I need to do this on every device?

Yes, once per device/app — adding it in Outlook on your laptop doesn't add it to Outlook Mobile automatically, and vice versa.

I'm a Manager/Admin — do I get anything extra?

Same steps to add it. Managers and Admins additionally get edit rights on the calendar itself once added, not just view.
