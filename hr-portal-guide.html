Holiday Request: the HR portal

How to look after everyone's holiday, sick and other leave, step by step. For HR and for anyone covering HR.

At a glance

Live

One place to see everyone's balance, record leave for people, and deal with requests when a manager can't.

What it is for. Everyone's allowance and what they have used; recording holiday, sick, unpaid and other leave on someone's behalf; approving, rejecting or cancelling a request for a manager; keeping bank holidays, the yearly rollover, an audit trail and exports.

Who can open it. Members of the HR group. People outside the group do not see it at all. Anyone who is added to the HR group also starts receiving HR's holiday emails.

Where to find it on the web. Open Holiday Request in your browser. HR members see two tabs at the top: My Requests and HR Portal. Choose HR Portal.

Where to find it on the phone. Open the Holiday Request tile in the AVT Master App. On My Requests, HR members see an HR portal button under + New Request. It opens a screen called "HR portal" with the subtitle "Everyone's holiday balance".

The holiday year. Most tabs have a year picker: ‹ and › either side of a label such as "2026-27". The year you pick is shared by People, Leave records, Bank holidays, Year rollover and Export. It starts on the 1 April on or before today.

Two things to know about the phoneThe Holiday tile on the phone opens only for people in one of the two team groups (LBG Support or Office & Installation), so an HR member who is in neither team can use the web portal but not the phone one. And if you have just been added to the HR group, sign out of the app and back in once before the button appears.

How balances work

Worth five minutes. Every number on the People grid and on a person's page comes from these rules.

The holiday year runs 1 April to 31 March. A leave record belongs to the year it starts in. Leave that starts in March and runs into April counts wholly in the earlier year, unless you split it (see Adding and editing leave).

Only Annual leave counts against the allowance. Sick, Unpaid and Other leave are added up separately and never reduce it.

Only Approved and Pending records count. Rejected and Cancelled records count for nothing.

Working days are stored on each record when it is saved. The balance adds up those stored numbers. Changing a bank holiday later does not change records already saved (use Recount for that).

The eight figures, in the order they appear
FigureHow it is worked out
EntitlementBase days plus all adjustments (positive or negative) for that year. Shows "—" if the person has no allowance for the year, does not track an allowance, or has no base days.
UsedWorking days of Approved Annual leave starting in the year.
PendingWorking days of Pending Annual leave starting in the year.
RemainingEntitlement minus Used. Pending is not taken off. Blank if Entitlement is blank.
AvailableRemaining minus Pending. The safe number to quote to someone. Blank if Remaining is blank.
Sick usedWorking days of Approved Sick leave.
Unpaid usedWorking days of Approved Unpaid leave.
Other usedWorking days of Approved Other leave.
Worked example Someone has a base of 25 days and a carry-over adjustment of 3. They have 12 days of approved holiday this year and a 5-day request still waiting.
Entitlement25 + 3 = 28
Used12
Pending5
Remaining28 − 12 = 16
Available16 − 5 = 11
How working days are counted

For any record, the portal counts Monday to Friday between the start and end dates, both dates included, and takes off any bank holiday for the person's region on those days. That includes both the official gov.uk bank holidays and any company days HR has added for that region.

Whole days only. There are no half days.

A record can be at most 60 days long (calendar days, counting both ends).

A person with no region set is counted as England and Wales.

People tab

The first tab, and the one the portal opens on. Everyone's balance for the chosen year, one row each.

The grid

Columns: Name, Department, Region, Type, Entitlement, Used, Pending, Remaining, Available, Sick, Unpaid, Other. Numbers show up to two decimal places; "—" means there is nothing to show (usually no allowance set yet).

Under a name you may see Needs review (their allowance for this year was created automatically and nobody has checked it), Set region (nobody has chosen their region yet; the Region column says "Not set"), No allowance tracked (their balance cells show "—" for that reason) or Inactive.

Click a row to open that person's page.

Search and "Show inactive"

The search box ("Search by name or email") narrows the list as you type. Inactive people are hidden until you tick Show inactive.

If the list is empty you will see "No people yet — the directory sync runs at 06:00; add a person manually if needed". If a search matches nobody: "No one matches that search."

+ Add person

For someone the daily sync with the staff directory has not picked up yet. Normally you will not need this: new team members appear by themselves each morning (see Starters and leavers).

Fields, in order: Name; Sign-in id (the 36-character id of the person's sign-in account; ask IT for it); Email; Department (LBG Support or Office & Installation); Region (England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland); Employment type (Employee, Contractor or Agency); tick boxes Tracks an allowance, Active and Approves their own leave; Start date (dd/mm/yyyy); Notes.

When the form opens, Employee, Tracks an allowance and Active are already set. Department and Region are blank and must be chosen.

Press Add person to save, or Cancel. On success you see "{Name} added."

Adding a person does not set their allowanceA new person has no allowance until you open their page and enter base days, so their Entitlement shows "—" until then. Nobody is emailed when you add a person. If the sign-in id is already in the system you will see "That person is already in the holiday system."

A person's page

Open it from the People grid, or with Open person on Leave records or Pending approvals. ‹ People takes you back.

At the top: the person's name; a line with their department, region and email; an Inactive chip if they have left; and the buttons + Add leave and + Add sick days. Green notices and red errors appear just under the header.

Year: the page shows the year chosen on the portal and has no year picker of its own. To look at another year, go back to a tab with a picker, change it, and open the person again.

Balance card ("Balance, 2026-27")

The eight figures explained in How balances work: Entitlement, Used, Pending, Remaining, Available, Sick used, Unpaid used, Other used.

Profile card

The name cannot be changed anywhere in the portal. For someone added with + Add person it is the name typed at the time, so check it before saving. You can change: Email; Department; Region; Employment type; the tick boxes Tracks an allowance, Active and Approves their own leave; Start date (dd/mm/yyyy); and Notes (up to 4000 characters). Press Save profile; you will see "Saved."

Region matters: it decides which bank holidays are taken off this person's leave. Department matters: it decides which team calendar their leave goes on.

Leaving Email or Start date blank keeps the current value; they cannot be cleared. Nobody is emailed when you save a profile.

Allowance card ("Allowance, 2026-27")

The status line reads "Base {x} + adjustments {y} = {z} days", or "No allowance recorded for 2026-27. Enter the base days to create it." if nothing is set yet, or "No allowance is tracked for this person" (shown when a row exists for someone who does not track an allowance; the Base days box is hidden for them).

Enter Base days (for example 25) and optional Allowance notes, then press Save allowance. Days must be between 0 and 365, with at most two decimal places. You will see "Allowance saved."

The review chip shows "Reviewed dd/mm/yyyy by {name}" or Not reviewed yet. Press Mark as reviewed to stamp your name and the date without changing anything. Any Save allowance also counts as a review, so either clears the "Needs review" chip on the People grid.

If the person does not track an allowance, saving stores no days and tells you: "This person does not track an allowance, so no days were stored."

Adjustments

Listed under the allowance as "{Kind} +n/-n — {reason} ({entered by})", or "None for 2026-27". Each one changes the Entitlement straight away.

To add one: Adjustment days (+ or −) (for example 3 or -1.5); Adjustment kind: Carry-over, Owed, Pro rata, Purchased, Correction or Other; Adjustment reason (required). Press Add adjustment. Days must be between -365 and 365, not zero, with at most two decimal places.

You must save the allowance first: the button stays off with the note "Save the allowance first; adjustments belong to it."

Remove next to an adjustment asks "Remove this adjustment? It comes off the allowance straight away." Adding or removing an adjustment does not change the review chip.

Leave ledger ("Leave in 2026-27")

Every leave record that starts in the chosen year. Each row shows the type, the dates, the number of working days, and chips for the status, the source (App for a request the person submitted, HR for leave HR entered), Note if there is an HR note, and Crosses 31 March if it does.

The note text itself and the person's reason are not shown in the ledger. The buttons you see depend only on where the record came from and its status; they do not depend on whether the dates have passed.

Edit

Opens "Edit leave for {name}" with the type, dates and HR note filled in. Saving rewrites the record and its calendar entries. The person, status and source never change. Only for HR-entered leave: an App request's dates cannot be edited (see Everyday recipes).

Delete

Asks "Delete this leave record? It is removed from the balance and from the calendar." Then it is gone for good, calendar entries included. The person is not emailed. The audit trail keeps the dates, type, days and status, but not the notes. On the phone the question reads "Delete this leave?" with Keep it / Delete.

Recount

No confirmation. Works out the working days again from the record's dates, using the person's current region and the current bank-holiday list, and saves the new number if it changed. You see "Recounted: N working day(s)." either way. Works on any status. Nobody is emailed. Records longer than the recount supports are refused: "That record is longer than the recount supports (61 days). Edit it instead." (such a record can only be fixed by editing an HR-entered one into shorter records; for an app request, contact IT).

Split

Only on HR-entered leave that crosses 31 March. Asks "Split this leave at 31 March into two records?" The original then ends on 31 March and a second record starts on 1 April, counting in the new holiday year. Both are recounted. You see "Split at 31 March: X + Y working days." If the second part cannot be created, the original is put back as it was. On the phone the question reads "Split this leave at 31 March?" with Keep it / Split.

Approve, Reject, Cancel request

Only on App requests. See Approving, rejecting and cancelling on someone's behalf.

Adding and editing leave

Recording holiday, sick days, unpaid leave or anything else on someone's behalf.

📝

Add leave for someone

Web or phone
  1. Open the personPeople tab, click their row. Check the year picker shows the year the leave starts in.
  2. Press + Add leave or + Add sick days+ Add leave opens "Add leave for {name}" with Annual leave chosen. + Add sick days opens the same form with Sick leave chosen.
  3. Choose the typeAnnual leave, Unpaid leave, Sick leave or Other. Only Annual leave comes off the allowance.
  4. Enter the datesTwo boxes, Start and End, typed as dd/mm/yyyy (for example 09/11/2026). A single day is the same date in both. A line under the dates shows the working-day count straight away, such as "3 working days". On the phone, Choose dates opens a date picker instead.
  5. Add an HR note if you need one"HR note (optional)", up to 2000 characters. It is for HR's eyes: it never goes on a calendar and the person does not see it. Some situations require a note (see below).
  6. SavePress Add leave (or Save changes when editing). You see "Leave added: N working day(s)." or "Saved: N working day(s)."
What gets createdThe record is saved as Approved, source HR, with your name on it. It goes on the person's department calendar as an all-day entry "{Name} - Annual Leave" (or Unpaid Leave, Sick Leave, Other), and also on the other team's calendar if the person is in that team's group or the cross-posting group. The calendar entry can take up to 15 minutes to show in Outlook. The note text is not put on the calendar.
The person is not toldLeave that HR enters sends the person nothing: no email, no calendar invite, no phone notification. It simply appears in their My Requests list marked "Entered by HR", and they cannot cancel it themselves. If they need to know, tell them.
The "Enter it anyway" boxes

Some things are blocked at first. When that happens the error appears and a tick box appears under it. Tick it, then press the button again. The box stays ticked for any further tries in the same form.

"Enter it anyway: it overlaps other leave" appears when the dates clash with Pending or Approved leave the person already has ("That overlaps leave this person already has. Confirm to enter it anyway."). An overlap also needs an HR note saying why, even though the note box says "optional". Without one you will see "An overlapping record needs a note saying why."

"Enter it anyway: no working days (sick days only, with a note)" appears when the range has no working days, for example a weekend ("That range has no working days. Only a sick day with a note can be entered as 0 days."). This is allowed only for Sick leave, and only with an HR note.

"Enter it anyway: this person is inactive" appears when the person has left ("That person is not active. Confirm to enter leave for an inactive person."). This is how you record leave for a leaver after the event.

Checks before it saves

You need a type and two real dates. The end date cannot be before the start date. Leave cannot run longer than 60 days. Notes are limited to 2000 characters. The working-day count shown on screen is a preview; the number the portal stores is its own count using the same rules.

Leave that crosses 31 March

If the dates cross 31 March, the form tells you: "This leave crosses 31 March. Save it as one record, or split it afterwards: the second part then counts in the new holiday year."

Saved as one record, all of it counts in the year it starts in. If you want the April days to come off next year's allowance, save it and then press Split on the ledger row. Split is only available on HR-entered leave.

Editing

Edit on an HR-entered row opens "Edit leave for {name}" with the type, dates and HR note filled in. Change what you need and press Save changes. The calendar entries are replaced and the working days recounted with the person's current region. The "Enter it anyway" boxes always start unticked, so an overlap or an inactive person will need confirming again.

Approving, rejecting and cancelling on someone's behalf

For requests people have submitted themselves. HR-entered leave is changed with Edit or Delete instead.

Where: the Pending approvals tab (every waiting request, all years) or the person's own ledger.

Which buttons: a Pending request has Approve, Reject and Cancel request. An Approved request has Cancel request only. Rejected and Cancelled requests have none.

Whichever comes first counts. The manager still has the request in their own approvals and email. If they have already answered, you will usually see "Only a pending request can be decided (it is Approved)." (or "…(it is Rejected)."); for a cancel, "Only a pending or approved request can be cancelled (it is …).". If they answer at the very moment you save, you see "Someone else changed this request while you were working. Reload to see where it stands." Either way nothing is changed and the list reloads. If you answer first, their later answer is ignored.

Leave records and Pending approvals

Two ways of seeing leave across everyone rather than one person at a time. Leave records is web only. Pending approvals is on the web and on the phone (the Pending approvals button at the top of the phone's HR portal screen).

Leave records: "All records"

Every record that starts in the chosen year, newest first, with the name, type, dates, working days and chips for the status, the source and "Crosses 31 March". Open person takes you to their page; there are no edit or decision buttons here.

The Filters card has: Type (Any / Annual leave / Unpaid leave / Sick leave / Other); Status (Any / Pending / Approved / Rejected / Cancelled); Source (Any / App / HR); "Starts from (dd/mm/yyyy)" and "Starts up to (dd/mm/yyyy)"; tick boxes "Crosses 31 March" and "Needs review (people whose allowance is not reviewed)"; and "Search by name or email".

Press Apply filters to run the Type, Status, Source, date and Crosses 31 March filters. The name search and the Needs review box narrow the list straight away without it. Clear filters resets everything.

The date filters match the start date of each record, not any overlap. Dates must be real or blank, and "to" cannot be before "from".

Leave records: "Who is off"

Switch the View to Who is off. It shows approved leave that has not yet ended, week by week (Monday to Sunday) from this week on, as cards titled "Week of dd/mm/yyyy". Weeks with nobody off are left out. Click a line to open the person. If nobody is booked off: "Nobody is booked off from today on in 2026-27".

Pending approvals

Every waiting request anyone has submitted, oldest first, from any year (this tab ignores the year picker). Each shows the name, type, dates, working days, the person's reason in quotes if they gave one, the chips listed under Approve, reject, cancel, and the buttons Approve, Reject, Cancel request and Open person. When there is nothing waiting: "Nothing waiting for approval".

Bank holidays tab

The days that are taken off working-day counts, by region.

Where the list comes from. The official gov.uk bank holidays for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland are pulled in automatically once a week. These rows carry a gov.uk chip and cannot be removed or edited here. Rows HR adds carry Added by HR.

One row covers one region. A company-wide day off needs a row for each region your people are in.

Under the title you will see "Last synced from gov.uk dd/mm/yyyy". That is the date the newest gov.uk holiday was added to the list, not the date of the last weekly check, so it does not move every week.

Year rollover

Creating next year's allowances, with carry-over, for everyone at once. Web only.

When. The tab opens on 1 February each year (before that it says "The rollover into 2027-28 opens on 01/02/2027."). On 1 March HR receives a reminder email headed "ROLLOVER DUE" listing anyone still to do. On 1 April anyone not yet rolled over is created automatically, with no carry-over.

Who. Active people only. Someone marked Inactive is not included.

The year picker shows the year you are rolling from; the line under the title reads "From 2026-27 into 2027-28".

Audit and Export

Seeing who changed what, and taking the data out as spreadsheets. Web only.

Audit tab

"The newest 200 changes. Notes and reasons are never stored here, only what changed." Every HR change, decision, cancellation, rollover and daily-sync change is recorded with what happened, who did it and when (UK time), and a one-line summary.

Filter by Kind of change (Any, Create, Update, Delete, Approve, Reject, Cancel, Entitlement, Adjustment, Rollover, Import, Staff, Recount, Bank holiday) or type in "Search the summary or who made the change". There is no filter by person or by date. Click a row to see the "Before" and "After" detail.

Deleted records keep a copy here with the notes and reasons blanked out, so a deleted record cannot be fully restored from the audit trail.

Export tab

Two buttons for the chosen year. Download people and balances gives holiday-people-2026-27.csv: one row per person (inactive people included) with name, email, department, region, employment type, active and tracks-allowance (Yes/No), base days, adjustments, and the eight balance figures. Download all leave records gives holiday-leave-2026-27.csv: every record starting in the year, any status, with name, email, type, status, source, start, end, working days, region used, approver, who decided and when.

Dates in the files are written year-month-day. HR notes, reasons and decision notes are not exported. The files open in Excel. You see "Downloaded {filename} (N rows)." when done.

ConfidentialAs the tab says: "These files contain names, emails and sick leave. Keep them with HR's other confidential records and delete them when you are finished."

New starters and leavers

What happens by itself every morning, and the parts HR must still do by hand.

Each morning at 06:00 the daily sync with the staff directory reads the two team groups (LBG Support and Office & Installation).

New team members are added automatically as Active, with the department of their team group (LBG Support if they are in both), no region, type Employee, tracking an allowance, and not approving their own leave. No allowance is created: their Entitlement shows "—". The directory cannot tell England and Wales from Scotland or Northern Ireland, so the region is left for you: the People grid shows "Not set" with a Set region chip, and the morning email asks you to choose one. Until you do, England and Wales bank holidays are used.

People who have dropped out of both groups are marked Inactive (only if the sync had seen them in a group before). Nobody is ever deleted. Someone HR has added by hand is never switched off before they have appeared in a group.

HR is emailed a summary ("Holiday staff directory: N new, M left") only on days when something changed. The sync does not reactivate someone who comes back, and does not update an existing person's name, email, department or region.

Safety rulesIf either team group comes back empty, or cannot be read, nothing changes. If more than 5 people appear to have left in one morning, nobody is deactivated; HR gets an email marked "CHECK GROUPS" asking you to check the groups and then deactivate real leavers in the portal yourselves. New starters are still added.

What HR does for a new starter

Web only
  1. Find them on the People tabThey appear the morning after they are added to their team group. Not there? Use + Add person.
  2. Set their allowanceOpen them, enter Base days on the Allowance card, press Save allowance. For a part-year starter, add a Pro rata adjustment with a reason rather than changing the base.
  3. Choose their RegionProfile card, choose England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland (the box starts empty, and the profile will not save until you choose), Save profile. Until you do, their leave is counted with England and Wales bank holidays, and adding leave for them warns "No region is set for this person, so England and Wales bank holidays were used." Recount any leave you entered before choosing.
  4. Check the rest of the profileDepartment, employment type, start date, and the tick boxes.

For a leaver: normally the sync marks them Inactive the morning after they leave the team group. To do it yourself, open them, untick Active, press Save profile. Inactive people are hidden from the People grid unless "Show inactive" is ticked, are left out of the year rollover, and still appear in the people export. You can still record leave for them with the "Enter it anyway: this person is inactive" box. To bring someone back, tick Active again and press Save profile: the daily sync then leaves them alone even if they are not in a team group, until they appear in one again.

Good to know

The things that catch people out. None of them are faults; they are just how the portal works today.

HR-entered leave sends the person nothing

No email, no invite, no notification. It does go on the shared team calendar, and a sick day shows there as "{Name} - Sick Leave". Approving, rejecting or cancelling a request on someone's behalf does email them.

An overlap needs a note

The note box says "optional", but if you tick "Enter it anyway: it overlaps other leave" without a note the save is refused. A 0-working-day sick day needs a note too.

The confirm boxes only appear after a refusal

Press the button, read the message, tick the box, press again.

Approving never checks the balance

Look at Available before you approve.

Approving or cancelling finished leave is allowed, with a warning

HR can still approve or cancel leave whose dates have passed (staff cannot cancel finished leave themselves). The decision box says "This leave has already finished." before you confirm an approve or a cancel, and the reply repeats it. Leave ending today has not finished.

HR cannot edit an App request's dates

Only Recount, Approve, Reject or Cancel. To change dates, cancel it with a reason and add HR leave.

Leave lands in the year it starts

The add form has no year field. If you are viewing 2026-27 and add leave starting in April 2027, it is saved but appears under 2027-28.

Recount is one record at a time

After a bank-holiday or region change, there is no "recount everyone" button. A recount changes only the stored days, not calendar entries: to refresh the calendar on HR-entered leave, use Edit.

"Last synced from gov.uk" is the date of the newest gov.uk row

Not the date of the last weekly check. A check that finds nothing new does not move it. gov.uk rows cannot be removed or edited from the portal.

New starters have no region and no allowance

Choose Region and enter Base days yourself. The People grid shows "Set region" until you do. Until then England and Wales bank holidays are used.

Who is off includes sick leave

It lists every approved type, so sick days show there too.

Open person can be greyed out

On Leave records and Pending approvals the button is off when the person is not in the holiday system for the chosen year.

The Audit search covers only what is loaded

It searches the newest 200 rows (of the chosen kind), not the whole history.

"Needs review" means an automatic row

In practice the rows created on 1 April. Rollovers you confirm and allowances you save are already reviewed. Any Save allowance clears the chip, not only Mark as reviewed.

A profile's email and start date cannot be cleared

Leaving either blank keeps the old value. Notes can be cleared.

Delete is permanent

The audit trail keeps the dates, type, days and status, but not the notes or reasons.

The Audit tab shows the newest 200 only

And filters by kind of change or free text, not by person or date.

Pending approvals ignores the year picker

It always shows every waiting request, oldest first.

A few date messages say yyyy-MM-dd

One or two messages mention dates as year-month-day even though you type dd/mm/yyyy on the web. On the phone, use the date picker.

Status key

Pending

Waiting for a decision. Counts in "Pending", not "Used". HR can Approve, Reject or Cancel it.

Approved

On the team calendar and counted in "Used" (Annual) or the Sick, Unpaid or Other totals. All HR-entered leave is Approved.

Rejected

Turned down. Counts for nothing. A closed record: it cannot be edited or split.

Cancelled

Withdrawn by the person or by HR. Counts for nothing. Also a closed record.

Common questions

Someone's Entitlement shows "—". Why?

They have no allowance for that year yet, do not track an allowance, or have no base days. Open them and enter Base days on the Allowance card.

Does a sick day come off the holiday allowance?

No. Only Annual leave does. Sick, Unpaid and Other are totalled separately.

Why has nothing changed after I added a bank holiday?

Records already saved keep their working days. Press Recount on each one that includes the date.

Will the person know I have recorded their leave?

Not from the portal. HR-entered leave sends no email or notification. It appears in their My Requests marked "Entered by HR". Tell them yourself if they need to know.

Can I change a request someone submitted from the app?

You can approve, reject or cancel it (with a reason they are sent), and recount it. You cannot change its dates: cancel it and add HR leave, or ask them to submit a new request.

What does the "Needs review" chip mean?

That year's allowance was created automatically (for example by the 1 April fallback) and nobody has checked it. Open the person, check the Base days, and either Save allowance or Mark as reviewed.

I am HR and I submitted my own request. Can I approve it?

Not if it has a manager on record. Ask another HR member, or wait for your manager.

I cannot see the HR portal button on my phone.

Sign out and back in once if you were added to the HR group recently. The Holiday tile itself only opens for people in one of the two team groups; if you are in neither, use the web portal.

Can I undo a Delete?

No. The audit trail records that it happened, with the dates, type, days and status, but the notes are gone. Add the leave again if it was a mistake.

Getting help

If a number looks wrong, check the person's ledger and adjustments first; most puzzles are a record in the wrong year, a missing allowance, or a count that needs a Recount.

If something still looks wrong, if you see an error that keeps coming back, or if you need someone added to or removed from the HR group, contact IT. Say which person and which year you were looking at, and what you pressed.

For requesting your own leave, see the main guide.
