2Common issues

  • Can't sign in — make sure you're using your company Microsoft account and have an active internet connection for the first sign-in.
  • Scan Room RFID not working — make sure NFC is enabled on your device and you're holding the phone close to the tag.
  • Missing app tiles on the home screen — access to each tool is controlled by your Microsoft security group membership. Contact IT if you believe you should have access to something you don't see.
