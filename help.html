LBG Room Checker

Live

Inspect AV equipment in meeting rooms — scan a room, run the checklist, log faults, and keep working offline.

Scan tab

Use Scan Room RFID when the device supports NFC/RFID — hold the phone close to the room tag, or Cancel to stop.

Manual Room Search is always available. Type a room name, RoomID, site, building, or floor. Clear resets the search.

Inspection tab

Once a room is selected, work through the checklist with Pass, Fault, or N/A — N/A counts as acceptable for the overall result.

Any item marked Fault needs fault details and, where required, photos before you can submit.

Room Clean and Ready is calculated automatically from your checklist answers.

Site Rooms tab

Pick a site, then a building, to see its cached rooms in RoomID order, matching how they're laid out physically. Rooms never checked — or overdue past the site's flag threshold — are marked so you can see what still needs doing. Tap a room to jump straight to Inspection. Works fully offline.

Sync tab

Refresh Offline Room Cache before working offline or assigning RFID data.

Sync Pending Checks uploads anything completed while offline, once you're back online.

Tools tab

Select a room from Scan first. Assign RFID Tag lets you scan and save a new tag for that room — it warns if the tag's already assigned elsewhere.

Troubleshooting

RFID can't find a room? Refresh the offline room cache and check the tag matches the room's stored ID.

Photos won't upload? Check you're online and the Fault Photos library is reachable.

Warehouse Asset

Live

Scan and track warehouse devices against the Asset Register — a set sequence from sync to complete.

  1. Sync SharePoint dataFrom Home, use Manual Sync SharePoint to download the latest Asset Register rows and upload any queued offline scans.
  2. Start scanningTap Start Scanning, then select Client, Job Name, Room Name, and Device.
  3. Enter device detailsEnter Serial Number, or use Set Serial as N/A if there isn't one. MAC Address, MAC Address 2, and MAC Address 3 are optional.
  4. Using OCRTap OCR next to a field to photograph and read the value automatically. A field scanned before pre-fills and appears under Previously Stored Values, so you can reuse it without rescanning.
  5. MAC address typeFor each MAC address entered, choose Control, Dante, Wifi, or Other.
  6. Review before completingUse Review Details to check Client, AVT Job Number, Job Name, Room Name, Device, Serial, MAC addresses/types, and Notes.
  7. Complete deviceTap Mark Device Complete to write the scan back to SharePoint. Completed devices show a green tick on Pick Device to Scan.
  8. Offline useOffline, the app uses cached SharePoint data. Completed scans queue locally and upload with Manual Sync once you're back online.

Holiday Request

Live

Request annual leave and track manager approval — from the app, or from your browser.

📱

From the app

Recommended
  1. Open the tileSign in to AVT Master App, then tap Holiday Request on the Launcher.
  2. Start a new requestTap + New Request. Set Start Date and End Date, pick a Leave Type (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Unpaid Leave, or Other), add a reason if you'd like, then Submit.
  3. Track itMy Requests lists everything you've submitted with a status badge.
  4. Change your mind?Open the request and tap Cancel Request — works on anything Pending or already Approved, calendar entry included.
Don't see the tile?Access is set per team. Contact IT to be added to your team's group.
💻

From your browser (the Form)

No app needed
  1. Find the formIn Edge or Chrome: Favourites bar → AV-Team Favourite folder → pick the form for your team — LBG Support or Office & Installation.
  2. Fill it inSign in with your AV-Team account if prompted, then enter your name, dates, leave type, and reason.
  3. SubmitIt goes into the exact same system as an app-submitted request, and follows the exact same approval.
Use the right form for your teamThe Form can't show you its status or cancel it afterwards — for that, use the app, or contact your manager or IT.
What happens after you submit

Your request goes to your line manager by email to Approve or Reject, with a reminder roughly every 24 hours until they decide.

Approved: you get an email confirming it and it's added to the team calendar automatically.

Rejected: you get an email too, with a reason if one was given.

No manager on record? IT is notified automatically so it doesn't sit unactioned.

Status key
Pending

Waiting on your manager.

Approved

Confirmed, on the team calendar.

Rejected

Not approved — see the email for why.

Cancelled

Withdrawn by you, before or after approval.

Common questions
Which Form do I use?

Whichever matches your team — LBG Support or Office & Installation. Same split as the app tile.

I submitted the wrong dates — what now?

From the app: cancel it and submit a new one. From the Form: contact your manager or IT.

Haven't heard back yet?

Your manager gets a daily reminder until they respond. If it's been a while, feel free to chase them directly too.

Does a Rejected request need resubmitting?

Yes — check the reason in the email and submit a fresh request if you still need the time off.
